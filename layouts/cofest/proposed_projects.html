📋 Cofest Proposed Projects

Explore project ideas for the GBCC 2025 Cofest, and share your own ideas using this Google Form!

🤔 What is a Cofest Project?

A Cofest Project is not necessarily a specific achievable goal, but rather a collaborative effort to explore a topic, share knowledge, write code, develop a tool or in general, work together on something that interests you.

A good project idea for the Cofest is preferrably a collaborative one or at the very least, one that would benefit from in-person planning, feedback and dicussion.

📝 What Kinds of Projects Can You Expect?

We encourage you to suggest your own ideas, but here are some examples of projects that have been proposed, which you can join or use as inspiration:

📦 Packaging an analysis using R shiny

Work with Galaxy developers to package an analysis using R shiny and deploy it on Galaxy.

🔧 Tool Wrapping

Wrap a tool for Galaxy or Bioconductor, making it easier to use and integrate.

📦 Bioconductor Package Development

Got a package idea, already maintaining one, or just eager to contribute? Whether you're looking for feedback and guidance, or are eager to dive in and become a maintainer yourself, this is your chance to connect with Bioconductor developers, learn, and contribute to the Bioconductor ecosystem.

📝 Tool YAMLs and Extending Support

Explore how to create and extend tool YAMLs for better compatibility.

🤖 Galaxy Machine Learning Tools

Develop and integrate machine learning tools into the Galaxy framework.

🔧 Bioconductor Package Dependency Optimization

Discuss and provide tooling to manage import chain complexity for Bioconductor packages.

💬 ChatGXY

Work with Galaxy developers on improving the newly introduced ChatGXY (AI assistant) integrated into Galaxy.

🐛 Galaxy Papercuts (Bug Fixes)

Get hands-on attention from a regular Galaxy developer, get Galaxy running locally, find an issue to work on and resolve it!

📄 Bioconductor Papercuts: Bring Your Questions, Bugs, or Suggestions

Meet and talk to Bioconductor developers, to ask questions, bring attention to bugs, or suggest changes/features from Bioconductor that would improve your experience using/developing Bioconductor.

📢 How to Work on a Cofest Project

We will compile a list of projects based on the suggestions you provide through the Google Form. At the start of the Cofest, we will come together to share these projects, along with additional recommendations and the designated point of contact or project lead for each. During the event, you can connect with them and begin collaborating!

Don't worry, we'll make sure there's something for everyone to work on.
