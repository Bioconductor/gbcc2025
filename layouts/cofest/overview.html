✨ What is CoFest?

A hands-on, interactive event where everyone can contribute to the Galaxy & Bioconductor communities!

🚀 CoFest: A Collaborative Sprint for Everyone

CoFest is a community-driven event where participants work together on exciting projects related to Galaxy and Bioconductor. It’s similar to a hackathon, but it's not just for programmers — it’s for anyone who wants to contribute to the ecosystem!

Whether you're a developer, scientist, educator, writer, or designer, there’s a place for you at CoFest!

🤝 What to Expect at CoFest

CoFest is highly interactive and informal—you’ll work side by side with others who share your interests! Here’s what you can look forward to:

  • 🔧 Improve Tools & Workflows – Help enhance Galaxy & Bioconductor tools and functionalities.
  • 📖 Enhance Documentation & Training – Make it easier for others to use and learn these platforms.
  • 🎨 Design & User Experience – Improve accessibility, visuals, and usability.
  • 📢 Community Discussions – Connect with like-minded people and explore new ideas.
  • 💡 No Pressure, Just Collaboration – Work at your own pace on projects that interest you.

🌍 Why Should You Join?

CoFest is more than just a gathering—it’s an opportunity to:

  • ✅ Meet Experts & Enthusiasts – Connect with the Galaxy & Bioconductor communities.
  • ✅ Learn New Skills – Get hands-on experience contributing to real-world projects.
  • ✅ Make an Impact – Your contributions will help improve the ecosystem for thousands of users.
  • ✅ Have Fun! – Work in a relaxed environment with passionate, supportive people.
