Scientific Program

Stay tuned! More details will be available soon.

We’re thrilled to announce the inaugural joint conference between BioConductor and the Galaxy Project! Although the final conference program is still being developed, we are excited to open abstract submissions now.

This unique conference brings together experts and enthusiasts from both communities, offering an unparalleled opportunity to explore cutting-edge research, innovative tools, and collaborative projects at the intersection of bioinformatics and data analysis. Whether you’re passionate about genomics, computational biology, or data-driven research, this event will feature:

  • Keynote presentations by thought leaders in the fields of bioinformatics and computational biology
  • In-depth research presentations showcasing the latest breakthroughs and methodologies
  • Hands-on workshops designed to enhance your technical skills
  • Networking sessions that foster collaboration and idea exchange

To join us, please register through Cold Spring Harbor. By clicking the button below, you will be redirected to their registration portal, where you can complete your registration and submit your abstract in one seamless process. Registration is required prior to abstract submission, but don’t worry—you can defer payment until your abstract has been accepted.

This conference is more than just an event—it’s a platform for building the future of bioinformatics and data science. Be part of a vibrant community, share your innovative ideas, and contribute to the evolution of research in these dynamic fields. We look forward to welcoming you and seeing the exciting contributions you have to offer!
