Abstract Submission Guidelines

Everything you need to know about submitting an abstract for GBCC 2025.

Submission Requirements

You must **register for the meeting** before submitting an abstract.

After registering, you will receive an **email with a web link** to submit your abstract.

Abstracts should be written in **plain text** and can be copied and pasted from **Word, Google Docs, or Notepad**.

Abstracts are limited to **~2900 characters** (including spaces).

Program Information

The **electronic abstract book** will be sent out **three working days before** the conference.

Printed copies will be available **upon arrival** at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory.

Only **keynote speakers and first-night presenters** will receive their session date and time in advance.

Other program details will only be available once the electronic abstract book is released.

This policy is designed to **maximize interactions** and encourage participants to stay for the full event.

Talk and Poster Information

Please check your **email** for details regarding:

  • Talk length and scheduling
  • Poster session instructions
  • Poster printing options at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory

For **poster printing**, CSHL offers an on-site service where your poster will be ready for collection upon arrival.
