GBCC 2025 Scholarship Application

Supporting graduate students & early-career researchers to attend GBCC 2025 at CSHL

Application Deadline: June 1, 2025

About the Scholarship

This scholarship supports graduate students and early-career researchers to participate in the Galaxy & Bioconductor Community Conference 2025 at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory in New York. We aim to empower outstanding individuals by offsetting costs, ensuring broad inclusivity and diverse participation.

Apply Now

Scholarship Tiers

  • Tier 1: Registration + travel support up to $700. Limited, highly competitive. A brief justification is required if you cannot attend without travel funding.
  • Tier 2: Registration only. For applicants who have secured their own travel funding—confirmation required.
  • Tier 3: Virtual conference registration only.

What Registration Includes (Tiers 1 & 2)

  • Full access to sessions, workshops, and networking events
  • Daily meals: breakfast, lunch & dinner
  • Welcome reception & banquet
  • On-campus housing
  • Free on-site parking

For more information on registration benefits, visit the conference registration page.

How to Apply

Before applying, you must first submit a poster abstract via our submission portal. As part of that process, please register for the conference and select “Check to follow” when asked how you will pay. After registering, you will receive an email with a link to submit your abstract.

Once registered, complete the scholarship application form before June 1, 2025.
