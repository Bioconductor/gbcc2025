Scholarships @ GBCC 2025

Applications are now closed — thank you to everyone who applied!

Please check back for future funding opportunities or contact us at gbcc2025-org@gaggle.email for virtual registration support.

Overview

GBCC 2025 offers two targeted awards to foster emerging talent in computational biology and open‑source science:

  • JXTX Scholarships — covers registration & travel
  • JJ Travel Fellowships — travel grants for students & postdocs

JXTX Scholarships

The JXTX Foundation will award four scholarships:

  • Tier 1 — registration + up to US$1,000 travel
  • Tier 2 — registration only
Apply for JXTX Scholarship

JJ Travel Fellowships

Two fellowships supporting registration & travel for graduate students or postdoctoral researchers.

Key Dates

  • Apply by: April 8 2025
  • Winners Announced: April 17 2025

How to Apply

Email the following to jj-fund@jxtxfoundation.org:

  • A 1‑page statement of research interests & conference goals
  • Your CV
  • An advisor’s reference letter
Apply for JJ Fellowship

Our Mission

These awards remove financial barriers and honor JJ’s legacy of mentorship, empowering the next generation of Galaxy & Bioconductor contributors.

About James “JJ” Johnson

JJ was a pioneering developer whose work at the Minnesota Supercomputing Institute shaped the Galaxy ecosystem and inspired countless scientists.
