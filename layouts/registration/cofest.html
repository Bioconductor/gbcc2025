Galaxy & Bioconductor CoFest 2025

A separate community event for collaborative development and contribution.

What is CoFest?

CoFest is a community gathering for individuals interested in contributing to Galaxy's & Bioconductor's ecosystem, including:

  • Developing and improving tools
  • Enhancing documentation & training materials
  • Expanding the code base

Participants work in an informal, highly interactive setting to collaborate around shared interests and address common challenges.
