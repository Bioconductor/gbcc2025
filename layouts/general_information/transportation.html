Getting to Cold Spring Harbor

Find the best way to travel to GBCC 2025 at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory.

🚆 By Train (From NYC)

The Long Island Rail Road (LIRR) runs between Moynihan Train Hall (Penn Station) in NYC and Syosset LIRR Station.

Free Shuttle: CSHL provides a complimentary shuttle service between Syosset and Grace Auditorium.

🚖 By Taxi (From Syosset LIRR & Local Areas)

Need a ride? Call a local taxi service:

  • Syosset Taxi: (516) 921-2141

✈️ By Plane

🛬 John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK)

45-90 min travel time to CSHL depending on traffic.

Take the JFK AirTrain to Jamaica Station, then connect to the LIRR.

🛬 LaGuardia Airport (LGA)

45-90 min travel time to CSHL.

Uber/Lyft or a reserved car is recommended.

🛬 Long Island MacArthur Airport (ISP)

40-60 min travel time to CSHL.

Uber/Lyft is recommended.

🚫 Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) (Not Recommended)

Up to 2 hours by car and expensive.

Alternative: Take the Newark AirTrain to Penn Station NYC, then transfer to the LIRR.

🚗 Recommended Car Services

For safe and reliable transportation, use one of these services:

  • Syosset Limousine Service (SLS) - (516) 364-9681 | Email
  • Limos Long Island - (516) 400-3364 (Use **promo code CSHL** for a discount)
  • Salmon Limousine Services - (631) 543-6412
  • Executive Limousine - (516) 826-8172 | Website

🛣️ Driving Directions

From NYC & Points West

Take I-495E (Long Island Expressway) → Exit 41N → Follow 107N → 106N → 25A to Bungtown Road (CSHL entrance).

From Points South

Take Southern State Pkwy → NY-135N → 25A → Bungtown Road (CSHL entrance).

From Points East

Take I-495W → NY-231 → 25A → Bungtown Road (CSHL entrance).
