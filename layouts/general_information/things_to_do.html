Explore & Enjoy: Things to Do Around Cold Spring Harbor

Whether you’re looking to unwind after a full day of conference activities or explore the natural beauty and history of the area, we’ve got you covered.

View CSHL Campus Map

On Campus Adventures

Exercise & Recreation

Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory offers a variety of recreational facilities, including hiking & jogging trails, courts, pool, kayaks & sailboats, and a fitness center.

Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory Tours

Guided walking tours led by CSHL graduate students and postdocs, exploring our Nobel-legacy campus and research history.

Off Campus Adventures

Cold Spring Harbor State Park

This 47-acre park (95 Harbor Rd) features hilly trails and harbor views—ideal for hiking and birdwatching.

Cold Spring Harbor Fish Hatchery & Aquarium

A state trout hatchery turned nonprofit aquarium with live exhibits, outdoor ponds, and catch-and-keep fishing.

The Whaling Museum & Education Center

Discover 6,000 artifacts—whaleboats, scrimshaw, maritime art—and year-round educational programs.

Cold Spring Harbor Library & Environmental Center

Located at 95 Harbor Rd, this library offers special collections on local natural history, plus community programs.

The Gourmet Whaler

Coffee shop and café at 111 Main St—famous for fresh-baked goods, award-winning fish tacos, and cozy vibes.
