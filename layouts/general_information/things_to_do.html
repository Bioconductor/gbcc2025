Explore & Enjoy: Things to Do Around Cold Spring Harbor

Whether you're looking to unwind after a full day of conference activities or explore the natural beauty and history of the area, we've got you covered! Here are some fantastic things to do both on and off campus.

🏛️ On Campus Adventures

🏃 Exercise & Recreation

CSHL offers a variety of recreational facilities including:

  • Hiking, walking, and jogging trails
  • Volleyball & tennis courts
  • Kayaks & sailboats (Memorial Day–Labor Day, 12-6 PM)
  • Swimming pool (Banbury campus only)
  • Indoor fitness center

For more details, inquire at the **Meetings & Courses Office**.

🔬 Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory Tours

Take a **guided walking tour** of the laboratory and explore its **history, architecture, and research** in areas like **cancer, neuroscience, and plant biology**. Tours are led by **graduate students and postdoctoral fellows**.

🌿 Off-Campus Adventures

🌳 Cold Spring Harbor State Park (1 mile away)

This **47-acre** park features hilly terrains and scenic views of the harbor. It's perfect for **hiking, birdwatching, and soaking in nature**.

🐟 Cold Spring Harbor Fish Hatchery & Aquarium (0.2 miles)

Originally a trout hatchery from **1883**, it now serves as an **educational center** with **freshwater reptiles, fish, amphibians**, and a **catch-and-keep trout fishing area**.

⚓ The Whaling Museum & Education Center (1.4 miles)

Dedicated to Long Island’s **maritime heritage**, this museum houses a **19th-century whaleboat**, **scrimshaw collections**, and year-round **exhibits & workshops**.

📚 Cold Spring Harbor Library and Environmental Center (1 mile)

Located at **95 Harbor Road**, this library serves Cold Spring Harbor, Laurel Hollow, and Lloyd Harbor with great **reading rooms and educational programs**.

☕ Local Shops & Eateries (1.5 miles)

Stroll through **Main Street** and enjoy local gems like **specialty boutiques, antique stores, and delicious eats** at places like the **Gourmet Whaler**.
