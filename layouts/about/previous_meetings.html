Galaxy Community Conferences

The Galaxy Community Conferences (GCCs) bring together researchers, developers, and educators from across the world. Recent highlights include:

  • Workshops on advanced bioinformatics tools and workflows.
  • Engaging keynote talks from industry leaders in genomics and data science.
  • Collaborative "Birds of a Feather" sessions promoting innovation and community building.

Bioconductor Conferences

The Bioconductor Conferences (BioC) are the premier events for open-source software in computational biology. Highlights include:

  • Hands-on tutorials for R packages like DESeq2, edgeR, and Shiny.
  • Panel discussions on reproducibility and the future of bioinformatics.
  • Networking sessions with developers and researchers from around the globe.
