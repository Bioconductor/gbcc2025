🌍 Welcome to GBCC 2025!

The Galaxy Bioconductor Community Conference (GBCC) is the first joint conference for the Galaxy and Bioconductor communities, bringing together scientists, software developers, educators, and researchers worldwide. GBCC fosters collaboration at the intersection of computational science, data analysis, and open science, enabling discoveries in diverse fields such as genomics, biomedical research, environmental modeling, cheminformatics, imaging, machine learning, and high-performance computing. By uniting these two leading open-source platforms, GBCC showcases how Galaxy and Bioconductor empower data-driven research across a wide range of computational and scientific disciplines.

📅 Conference Dates & Location

GBCC 2025 will take place from Monday, June 23, to Thursday, June 26, 2025, at the prestigious Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory (CSHL), New York.

The conference will kick off with dinner and an evening session on Monday and conclude with lunch on Thursday.

💡 Why Attend GBCC 2025?

GBCC is not just another conference — it’s a dynamic, interactive, and inclusive gathering that fosters collaboration and innovation. Whether you are a researcher, developer, or educator, GBCC provides immense value:

  • 🔬 Stay Ahead in Computational Science – Learn about the latest advancements in Galaxy and Bioconductor.
  • 🤝 Network with Experts & Peers – Meet and collaborate with leading scientists, developers, and trainers.
  • 🛠️ Hands-On Training – Gain practical skills through interactive workshops and tutorials.
  • 📢 Showcase Your Work – Present your research, tools, and workflows to an engaged audience.
  • 🚀 Contribute to the Community – Help advance open-source tools that drive innovation in computational research.
