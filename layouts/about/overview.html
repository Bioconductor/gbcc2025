Welcome to GBCC 2025

June 23–26, 2025 at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, New York

About GBCC 2025

The Galaxy Bioconductor Community Conference (GBCC) is the first joint meeting of two leading open‑source platforms, uniting Galaxy’s powerful data‑analysis framework with Bioconductor’s rich bioinformatics ecosystem. Over four days, you’ll engage with scientists, developers, and educators to explore cutting‑edge methods in genomics, imaging, machine learning, and high‑performance computing. Through keynote talks, hands‑on trainings, workshops, and Birds of a Feather sessions, GBCC empowers you to accelerate your research and broaden your collaborations. Experience this vibrant intersection of open science at world-renowned Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory.

Why Attend GBCC 2025?

GBCC brings together the best of Galaxy and Bioconductor to offer an unparalleled experience in learning, collaboration, and innovation:

  • World‑Class Keynotes – Be inspired by pioneering researchers who are shaping the future of data‑driven science.
  • Hands‑On Workshops – Gain practical expertise in integrating Galaxy and Bioconductor to accelerate your own research.
  • Poster Sessions – Present your work, exchange ideas, and receive constructive feedback from a global audience.
  • Birds of a Feather – Engage in focused discussions on topics ranging from metagenomics to software security best practices.
  • Collaborative CoFest – Join a supportive sprint where seasoned developers and newcomers collaborate to enhance community tools and workflows.

Connect in person or online with hundreds of peers, learn from community-driven trainings, and forge relationships with leading scientists and open‑source developers.
