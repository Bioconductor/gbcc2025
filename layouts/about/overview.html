About GBCC 2025

The Galaxy Bioconductor Community Conference (GBCC) is the first joint meeting of two leading open‑source platforms, uniting Galaxy’s data‑analysis framework with Bioconductor’s bioinformatics ecosystem. Over four days, engage with scientists, developers, and educators exploring genomics, imaging, machine learning, and high‑performance computing.

Through keynotes, hands‑on workshops, and community sprints, GBCC empowers you to accelerate your research and forge lasting collaborations.

Why Attend GBCC 2025?

  • Inspiring Keynotes — Learn from leaders at the forefront of data‑driven science.
  • Hands‑On Workshops — Master Galaxy & Bioconductor workflows directly.
  • Poster Sessions — Showcase your work and receive live peer feedback.
  • Focused Discussions — Deep dive into topics from metagenomics to software security.
  • Community Sprint — Collaborate on tools & materials, regardless of coding skill.
