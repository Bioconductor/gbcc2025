About Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory

Founded in 1890, CSHL has driven revolutionary advances in genetics, cancer biology, and neuroscience for over 130 years. Its researchers first unraveled DNA’s structure here, setting the stage for modern molecular biology. Today, its scenic waterfront campus blends historic brick halls with cutting‑edge labs, creating an inspiring setting for collaboration. Whether you’re wandering the iconic greenhouses or exploring interactive exhibits, you’ll find fresh ideas at every turn.

Why CSHL?

For more than a century, CSHL has not only produced groundbreaking science but has shaped how researchers convene worldwide. Highlights include:

  • Founding (1890): Over 130 years of continuous scientific gatherings.
  • Symposia (1933): Launched the world‑famous Quantitative Biology meetings and the Lab Press.
  • Banbury Center: Offers elite, small‑group workshops that pioneer interdisciplinary breakthroughs.
  • Meetings & Courses: Hosts 60+ events yearly, drawing ~9,000 scientists to Long Island.
  • Nobel Legacy: Eight laureates (Watson, Crick, McClintock…) presenting landmark discoveries here.

Each year, over 12,000 scientists from more than 50 countries choose CSHL—voted among the top conferences for scientific rigor, networking, and inspiration.
