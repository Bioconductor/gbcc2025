About Cold Spring Harbor

Located on Long Island, New York, Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory is a world-renowned center for research and education in biology and genetics. The campus offers state-of-the-art facilities, beautiful surroundings, and a vibrant atmosphere for collaboration and innovation.

The conference will take place in various venues across the campus, providing attendees with the opportunity to learn, network, and exchange ideas in an inspiring setting.
