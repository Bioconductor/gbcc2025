🦜 Birds of a Feather (BoFs) at GBCC 2025

Informal, interactive, and thought-provoking discussions on topics that matter to you.

🔍 What Are Birds of a Feather (BoFs)?

Birds of a Feather (BoFs) are free-form, participant-driven sessions that provide a space for rapid discussion and idea-sharing on topics of shared interest. They can be technical, casual, or even activity-based—whatever sparks your curiosity!

Unlike formal presentations, BoFs are conversational — anyone can suggest topics, join discussions, or just listen in.

🤝 Why Should You Join a BoF?

BoFs are a fantastic opportunity to:

  • 💡 Engage in meaningful discussions – Dive deep into topics with like-minded participants.
  • ⚡ Get quick insights – Unlike structured sessions, BoFs allow for rapid idea exchange.
  • 🛠️ Collaborate & brainstorm – Find potential collaborators or get feedback on projects.
  • 🎉 Have fun! – BoFs can be social, interactive, and even spontaneous.

📝 What Kinds of BoFs Can You Expect?

BoFs cover a wide range of topics, from technical deep dives to fun community activities. Here are just a few possibilities:

🌍 Bioinformatics & Tools

Discuss differences in bioinformatics tools for microbial analysis and explore new methodologies.

🖥️ Backend Computing Systems

Share insights into high-performance computing, workflow automation, and system design.

🏛️ Campus Tours

Explore Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory with a guided group walk.

📢 Open Science & Community Engagement

Talk about open-source collaboration, documentation, and training efforts.

🧑‍🏫 Education & Teaching Strategies

Exchange ideas on how to better teach computational biology.

🎮 Fun & Networking

From board games to social meetups—not all BoFs are technical!

📢 How to Participate in a BoF

BoFs are open to everyone, and you don’t need to register in advance. Just show up and join a conversation!

Want to propose a BoF session? Stay tuned for an upcoming BoF proposal form where you can suggest and lead discussions.
