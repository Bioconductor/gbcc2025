## Getting Here

### By Train
**From NYC**  
- LIRR from Moynihan Train Hall (Penn Station) to Syosset LIRR station.  
- Complimentary shuttle from Syosset to CSHL.

### By Taxi
**From Syosset LIRR (local)**  
- Syosset Taxi: (516) 921-2141

### By Plane
**JFK International Airport** (45–90 min)  
JFK AirTrain to Jamaica in Queens → LIRR to Syosset.  

**LaGuardia Airport** (45–90 min)  
Reserve car or rideshare.  

**MacArthur Airport** (40–60 min)  
Uber/Lyft recommended.  

**Newark (EWR)** (not recommended)  
Up to 2 hours. Use Newark AirTrain → Penn Station → LIRR.

### Recommended Car Services
- **Syosset Limousine Service (SLS):** (516) 364-9681  
  *2025 Rates, email [syossetlimo@aol.com](mailto:syossetlimo@aol.com)*

- **Limos Long Island:** (516) 400-3364  
  2025 Rates (use promo code CSHL)

- **Salmon Limousine Services:** (631) 543-6412
