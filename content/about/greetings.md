Dear Colleagues,

Welcome to the very first joint conference uniting the Bioconductor and Galaxy Project communities, to be held in 2025. We are excited to bring together researchers, developers, and professionals from around the world to explore the latest breakthroughs, discuss emerging challenges, and foster new collaborations in the ever-evolving field of bioinformatics.

For many years, both Bioconductor and the Galaxy Project have served as leading open-source platforms, empowering scientists with cutting-edge tools to advance genomic research and data analysis. By merging these two vibrant communities under one roof, we aim to broaden the impact of open science, accelerate innovative discovery, and form a network that supports diverse approaches to computational biology.

Through a carefully designed program of keynote sessions, hands-on workshops, and interactive panels, this event will offer a unique opportunity to exchange expertise and cultivate partnerships spanning multiple disciplines. Whether you are a seasoned developer, a curious newcomer, or an established investigator, you will find valuable insights and practical knowledge to elevate your research and professional growth.

We look forward to your enthusiastic participation in shaping the future of bioinformatics. Let us come together and forge new frontiers in data-driven science—one collaborative effort at a time.

Thank you, and we eagerly await welcoming you in 2025.
